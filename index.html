FAQ

Common questions

Is this coaching or therapy?
Coaching is future-focused. We work on goals, mindset, and action. I'm not a therapist and don't treat clinical conditions. If I think therapy would benefit you more, I'll be upfront about it.
How long before I see results?
Most clients experience a mindset shift within the first 2-3 sessions. Tangible life changes typically happen within 60-90 days, depending on your commitment level.
What if I'm not sure coaching is right for me?
That's exactly what the free Discovery Call is for. We'll chat for 30 minutes, I'll learn about your situation, and we'll both decide if it's a fit. Zero pressure, zero obligation.
Do you work with men and women?
Absolutely. My clients are roughly 60/40 women to men, ranging from age 25 to 60+. What they all have in common is a desire for meaningful change and a willingness to do the work.
What's your refund policy?
